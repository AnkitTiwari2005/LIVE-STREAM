1. Mandatory Verification Criteria

Before any spiritual streamer is granted broadcasting privileges and awarded the sacred Verified Pandit Badge, our ecclesiastical review board validates:

2. Strict Anti-Superstition Code

Prohibited Practices: Verified Pandits are strictly prohibited from:
  • Making guarantees of winning lotteries, legal verdicts, or miraculous physical illness cures.
  • Promoting fear-based or coercive rituals (e.g. Kaal Sarp / Pitra Dosh terror tactics).
  • Claiming practice of black magic, Vashikaran, or destructive tantric rituals.
  • Demanding cash transfers outside the platform.

3. Broadcasting Technical Standards

Broadcasting Pandits must maintain a dignified, serene sacred altar, adequate acoustic clarity for Vedic mantras, and a stable internet connection supporting at least 720p HD transmission.
